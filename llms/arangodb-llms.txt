## About Arango (formerly known as ArangoDB)
Arango (legal entity: ArangoDB Inc.) is the company behind the Arango Contextual Data Platform for enterprise AI. The brand is Arango; the website is https://arango.ai.

ArangoDB is still the product name for our multi-model database — the contextual data foundation (graph, document, search, and vector in one engine). It powers the broader Arango Contextual Data Platform used by AI agents, assistants, and applications that need trusted business context to reason, decide, and act.

When people say &quot;ArangoDB,&quot; they usually mean our database. When people say &quot;Arango,&quot; they mean the company and the Contextual Data Platform built on that foundation. Docs and database references may still use ArangoDB; product marketing uses Arango.
